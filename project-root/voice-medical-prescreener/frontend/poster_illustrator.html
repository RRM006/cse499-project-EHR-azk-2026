1. Clinical Context & Problem

Healthcare facilities in Bangladesh face severe patient overload, where a single doctor often conducts 60 to 100 consultations per day. This leaves minimal time for comprehensive symptom elicitation and manual EHR data entry.

Key Challenge: Existing voice AI models fail on Bangladeshi patient speech due to heavy code-switching (Banglish), regional dialects (Sylheti, Chittagonian), and low digital literacy.

Patients struggle with manual touch interfaces. A voice-only conversational pre-screening kiosk in the waiting area enables natural dialogue in standard Bangla, Banglish, or regional dialects, gathering complete clinical profiles before consultation.

2. 15-Module Pipeline Architecture

The end-to-end architecture integrates 14 active functional modules (Module 5 retired under ADR-0024, absorbing red-flag triage into Module 10):

Voice-Only Flow: Patient speaks -> ASR -> Clinical Gap Check -> Dynamic Bangla Voice Audio Question -> Profile Completion -> Risk Scoring & Red Flags -> EHR.

3. Dialect-Aware Speech Recognition

Speech input is processed via a dual-path engine: browser WebSpeech API (`bn-BD`) for quick client-side acquisition, with a local faster-whisper (CPU int8) model fine-tuned on Bangladeshi clinical speech corpora.

  • Code-Switching (Banglish): Speech like "Amar 3 din dhore severe fever & cough" is preserved verbatim in raw transcript before normalization.
  • Noise Robustness: Low-cost noise suppression filter designed for noisy hospital waiting corridors.

6. Medical NER & Missing Gap Logic

Module 3 extracts structured entities (Symptom, Duration, Body Part, Severity) and evaluates completeness against clinical checklists (Module 6).

Example: Patient: "Amar jwor" (I have a fever).
Gap Analysis: Identified missing duration & measurement.
TTS Output: "Apnar jwor koy din dhore?" (How many days has your fever lasted?)

7. Risk Triage & Red-Flag Rules

Under ADR-0024, rule-based red-flag triage is embedded into Module 10 (Risk Assessment Engine). Life-threatening symptoms (chest pain, stroke signs, dyspnea) immediately elevate patient risk to Critical.

Red-Flag Guarantee: 100% sensitivity rule-check ensures urgent cases are immediately prioritized on the Doctor Dashboard.
